## Series Arc for This Block

**Eps 26–32 (Cognitive Science Turn):** Naturalising relevance realization (RR)
as the core of intelligence, meaning-making, and spirituality. Culminates in a
neural implementation via self-organized criticality + small-world networks.

**Eps 33–35 (Spirituality of RR):** RR generates religio, wonder, awe, mystery,
sacredness. Introduces the symbol as the participatory vehicle for engaging the
sacred.

**Eps 36–40 (Constructive Proposal I):** Defines the two-component solution to
the meaning crisis: (1) ecology of psychotechnologies addressing perennial
problems, (2) new worldview legitimating meaning-making. Proposes "a religion
that isn't a religion."

**Eps 41–46 (Wisdom / Constructive II):** Cognitive science of wisdom. The
hierarchy Intelligence → Rationality → Wisdom. Vervaeke & Ferraro model. Wisdom
= integrated cultivation of AOM + mindfulness + internalizing the sage + four
kinds of knowing + aspiration + transformative experience + wonder.

**Eps 47–50 (Prophets):** Heidegger (aletheia, being-mode, Dasein), Corbin
(imaginal, divine double), Jung (individuation, Self, synchronicity), Tillich
(ultimate concern, ground of being, courage), Barfield (Original/Final
Participation).

## Vervaeke's Full Constructive Proposal (synthesized from eps 36–50)

The meaning crisis has two components that require two-component responses:

### 1. Address the perennial problems via an ecology of psychotechnologies

| Perennial Problem | Psychotechnology |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Parasitic processing (self-deception) | Counteractive dynamical system (mindfulness + AOM) |
| Modal confusion (having vs. being mode) | Cultivation of *sati* (mindful attentiveness to present mode) |
| Reflectiveness gap | Cultivation of *flow* (optimal engagement bypassing self-referential interference) |
| Absurdity | Cultivation of *prajñā* (insight wisdom) |
| Anxiety | Cultivation of inner dialogue — internalizing the sage |
| Alienation | Cultivation of *communitas* (authentic relating, circling) |
| Existential entrapment | Cultivation of *gnosis* through higher states of consciousness |

These must be integrated within a **wisdom framing** (not just practiced piecemeal) and cultivated within **communities of practice**.

### 2. Provide a new worldview legitimating meaning-making

- Grounded in 4E cognitive science + naturalized RR
- Recovers the nomological order (nature is an arena for agency) and normative order (values are real and action-guiding)
- Uses "transcendence into" (Goodenough) rather than "transcendence above"
- Structured as a "religion that isn't a religion": open-ended mythos, credo in service of religio, participatory and decentralized

### The ultimate aim: Final Participation (Barfield)

Freely chosen, consciously achieved re-engagement with the participatory depths of reality — recovering the connectivity that was lost through the meaning crisis, but recovered *through* reflective consciousness, not by bypassing it.
