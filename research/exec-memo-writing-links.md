## SearXNG Engine Notes

The local SearXNG instance (port 8888) returned useful results only from targeted engine subsets:
- **Semantic Scholar** and **Google Scholar** — productive for academic papers
- **Bing / DuckDuckGo** — returned geolocalised non-English results; unreliable for English-language queries without language headers
- **Brave News / Reuters / Google News** — returned 0 results for exec writing queries (topic too niche for news engines)
- **GitHub engine** — returned 0 results for exec memo queries (too niche)

Best-performing query pattern: `engines=semantic+scholar,google+scholar&language=en`
